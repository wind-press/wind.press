[Elementor](https://be.elementor.com/visit/?bta=209150&brand=elementor) is the most popular website building platform for WordPress with over 5 million active installs, enabling web creators to build professional, pixel-perfect websites with an intuitive visual builder.

## Using custom fonts in the Elementor

It's easy to use [Adobe Fonts](/docs/fonts/adobe-fonts) or [self-hosting Google Fonts](/docs/fonts/google-fonts) in Elementor visual builder. You can use any fonts you have [added](/docs/fonts/custom-font) to Yabe Webfont.

### Step 1: Choose the text element

Choose the text element by clicking on the canvas or the Structure panel.

![Choose the text element in Elementor](/tutorial/integration/elementor/1.png)

### Step 2: Typography settings

1. On the Element's settings panel, click the :icon{name="lucide:contrast"} icon to open the **Style** tab.

![Open the Style tab](/tutorial/integration/elementor/2.png)

2. Click the :icon{name="lucide:type"} icon on the right of **Typography** label to open the **Typography** panel.

![Open the Typography panel](/tutorial/integration/elementor/3.png)

### Step 3: Choose the font

Click the **Family** dropdown list, then select the font family from the **Yabe Webfont** group list.

![Choose the font family](/tutorial/integration/elementor/4.png)

## Using custom fonts in the Theme Styles

You can also use custom fonts in the Theme Styles. Theme Styles is a global style setting that allows you to set the default style for any text elements in the Elementor.

### Step 1: Typography settings

1. Click the :icon{name="lucide:sliders-horizontal"} icon on the top left corner of the Elementor visual builder to open the **Site Settings** panel.

![Open the Site Settings panel](/tutorial/integration/elementor/5.png)

2. Click the **Typography** menu item under the **Theme Style** section.

![Typography menu under Theme Style](/tutorial/integration/elementor/6.png)

3. Choose the element section and click the :icon{name="lucide:type"} icon on the right of **Typography** label to open the **Typography** panel.

![Open the Typography panel](/tutorial/integration/elementor/7.png)

### Step 2: Choose the font

Click the **Family** dropdown list, then select the font family from the **Yabe Webfont** group list.

![Choose the font family](/tutorial/integration/elementor/8.png)

## Using custom fonts in the Design System

You can also use custom fonts in the Design System. It has similar steps as using [custom fonts in the Theme Style](#using-custom-fonts-in-the-theme-styles).

### Step 1: Typography settings

1. Click the :icon{name="lucide:sliders-horizontal"} icon on the top left corner of the Elementor visual builder to open the **Site Settings** panel.

![Open the Site Settings panel](/tutorial/integration/elementor/5.png)

2. Click the **Global Fonts** menu item under the **Design System** section.

![Global Fonts menu under Design System](/tutorial/integration/elementor/9.png)

3. Choose the item and click the :icon{name="lucide:type"} icon on the right of **Typography** label to open the **Typography** panel.

![Open the Typography panel](/tutorial/integration/elementor/10.png)

### Step 2: Choose the font

Click the **Family** dropdown list, then select the font family from the **Yabe Webfont** group list.

![Choose the font family](/tutorial/integration/elementor/11.png)

## Disable the Google Fonts in Elementor

Elementor uses the Google Fonts Web API to provide users with the Google Fonts collection. To be GDPR compliant, you must disable the Google Fonts in Elementor.

Yabe Webfont forcibly disables the Google Fonts in Elementor by taking reference from the [official documentation](https://developers.elementor.com/docs/hooks/print-google-fonts), so you don't need to do anything.
