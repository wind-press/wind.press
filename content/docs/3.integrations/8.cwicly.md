[Cwicly](https://cwicly.com/?ref=suabahasa) is an advanced professional design & block toolkit that integrates directly with the WordPress editor.

Yabe Webfont is the pioneer of 3rd-party solutions for custom fonts and self-hosting Google Fonts in the Cwicly.

## How to use custom fonts in the Cwicly

You can use any [Custom Font](/docs/fonts/custom-font), embedding [Adobe Fonts](/docs/fonts/adobe-fonts) or [self-hosting Google Fonts](/docs/fonts/google-fonts) in the Cwicly easily.

### Step 1: Choose the text element

Choose the text element by clicking on the canvas or the List View panel.

![Choose the text element in Cwicly](/tutorial/integration/cwicly/1.png)

### Step 2: Switch to the Typography tab

On the Element panel, click on the :icon{name="lucide:type"} icon to switch to the **Typography** tab.

![Switch to the Typography tab](/tutorial/integration/cwicly/2.png)

### Step 3: Disable the Google Fonts in Cwicly

Cwicly uses the Google Fonts Web API to provide users with the Google Fonts collection. To be GDPR compliant, you must disable the Google Fonts in Cwicly.

Ensure that the :icon{name="simple-icons:googlefonts"} icon is **not** active.

![Disable the Google Fonts in Cwicly](/tutorial/integration/cwicly/4.png)

### Step 4: Choose the font

Click the **Font** dropdown field to open the list, and select the font family from the **Local Fonts** or **Deprecated** group list.

![Choose the font family](/tutorial/integration/cwicly/3.png)
